Defaults filled into every new row

Lines are picked out by shape, not by position: a 6-digit line is the pincode, a 10-digit line is the mobile, a line with COD or Prepaid is the payment and the cost. Everything before the pincode becomes name and address, everything after becomes the product. City comes from the address text, state from the pincode. Lines like 30 rec are ignored. Order ID is left blank. Use All fields on any row to fill the other sheet columns.
